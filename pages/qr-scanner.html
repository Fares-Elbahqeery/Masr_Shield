QR Code Safety & Quishing Scanner

QR codes are increasingly abused in phishing schemes ("quishing") because their encoded data is opaque to human eyes. This tool provides an isolated inspection sandbox to upload QR code images, decode raw data strings, and evaluate links for threats before any browser navigation takes place.

Notice: QR decoding uses jsQR in the browser when available. If the library cannot be loaded, decoding is currently unavailable. Decoded URLs are never opened automatically.

Security Best Practice: Inspect Before Accessing

Never Scan and Blindly Proceed: Attackers often affix malicious QR stickers over legitimate restaurant menus, parking meters, or bank flyers. Decoded URLs must be verified against official domain registries before being entered in a browser. This application deliberately prevents automatic link opening.

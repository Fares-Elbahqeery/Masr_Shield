Why Do Detection Discrepancies Occur?

1. Tokenization & Morphology Fragmentation

Subword tokenizers trained predominantly on formal Arabic split colloquial prefixes (e.g., Egyptian progressive "بـ", future "حـ", negating "مش") into fragmented tokens, disrupting keyword embeddings.

2. Orthographic Invariance Failure

Omitting hamzas (e.g., "انشاء" vs "إنشاء", "ايداع" vs "إيداع") or swapping taa marbuta with haa often causes static keyword lists and regex detectors to miss obvious threat signals.

3. Colloquial Idiom Blindspots

Phishing triggers in Egyptian slang (such as "الفرصة متفوتهاش" or "الفلوس مستنياك") lack 1-to-1 keyword overlap with standard MSA training dictionaries, leading to false negatives.

Research Methodology & Meaning Verification

Preservation Verification Required: For experimental validity, meaning preservation between original and rewritten pairs must be confirmed through human expert annotation or validated semantic textual similarity (STS) thresholds. Consistency rate calculations in research benchmarks measure the percentage of semantically equivalent pairs where the classifier makes identical predictions.

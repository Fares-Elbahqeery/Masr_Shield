Screenshot & Image Scanner

Many deceptive communications arrive as images—such as WhatsApp conversation screenshots, fabricated banking receipts, or sponsored social media advertisements. This module provides a pipeline to intake image files, prepare them for Optical Character Recognition (OCR), and inspect the extracted Arabic text for phishing indicators.

Pipeline Status Notice: OCR runs in the browser using Tesseract.js when available. If the library cannot be loaded, OCR is currently unavailable and no text is fabricated.

Technical Considerations for Arabic OCR

  • Cursive Script Continuity: Arabic characters change shape based on their position (isolated, initial, medial, final), which requires character-level segmentation models to avoid ligature misrecognition.
  • Diacritics & Dot Placement: Misplaced or blurred dots (I'jam) can transform innocent words into entirely different vocabulary, introducing noise into subsequent scam classification.
  • Low-Resolution Compression: Social messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram) aggressively compress screenshots, degrading image quality and lowering OCR confidence.

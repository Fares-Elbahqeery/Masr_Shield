How It Works

A transparent four-step workflow for evaluating suspicious digital content.

  1. 1. Submit Suspicious Content

    Input anonymized message text, enter a suspicious web link, or upload an image containing text or a QR code into the appropriate module.

  2. 2. Examine Potential Risk Indicators

    Inspect heuristic signals such as artificial urgency, prize notifications, authority impersonation, unfamiliar domain formats, or concealed redirection.

  3. 3. Review an Explainable Report

    Receive a transparent report that outlines why specific linguistic tokens or structural elements raised concern, along with explicit limitations.

  4. 4. Test Detection Consistency

    Use ConsistencyGuard to compare the original message against dialectal or paraphrased variants to evaluate whether detection decisions remain invariant.

Research Focus: Dialectal Arabic & Detection Robustness

Automated scam detection systems frequently suffer from significant performance degradation when processing informal Arabic. Attackers frequently bypass conventional keyword filters by shifting from Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) to colloquial regional dialects (such as Egyptian Arabic / العامية المصرية), adopting non-standard orthography (such as interchangeable alif and hamza characters, taa marbuta substitutions, or phonetic spelling), and paraphrasing coercive statements.

MasrShield investigates the resilience of security classifiers against these meaning-preserving variations. Our goal is to promote explainability, measure classification variance across dialects, and contribute to more dependable digital safeguards for Arabic-speaking communities.

Responsible Use Notice

Assessments Are Not Legal Proof

Risk scores and indicator summaries produced by this platform are assistive research signals, not legally binding proof of criminal fraud or conclusive guarantees of safety. Always verify sensitive interactions directly with official institutions.

Protect Confidential Information

Do not enter real passwords, one-time passcodes (OTPs), bank credentials, national identification numbers, or personal identifying information into any input field. Use anonymized or synthetic text examples when testing.

Experimental Validation Required

This platform represents an academic and technical research prototype. Detection accuracy, precision, and false-positive rates must be formally evaluated against standardized benchmark datasets before making any operational claims.
